## How to use the scribe worksheet

1. Open the **Your numbers** tab. The **Made-up example** tab shows the math with invented numbers.
2. Fill in the yellow cells for one clinician, or for a group's average: what the scribe costs all in, visits a month, collected per visit, documentation minutes per visit, and days to close a chart, before go-live and after.
3. Compare the same months before and after, a year apart if you can, and hold payer mix steady.
4. Read the three net payback lines. The last two are a stress test: what's left if payers take back part or all of the coding lift.

## Limits

These are measuring aids, not billing, legal or compliance advice. Confirm coding and payer contract questions with your compliance lead or counsel. The worksheets use only numbers your own practice holds, so they're only as good as the before figure you wrote down.
